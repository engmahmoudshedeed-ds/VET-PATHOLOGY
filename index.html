01 / INTRODUCTION

Movement is a biological masterpiece.

Muscles generate force; tendons transmit that force to bone; and ligaments stabilize joints by connecting bone to bone. Together, these tissues support locomotion, posture and mechanical integrity in animals.

Explore anatomy & functions →
⌁
Why Veterinary Pathology?

Pathology connects clinical signs with tissue-level injury. Gross examination and histopathology help identify the pattern, severity and cause of musculoskeletal disease.

02 / ANATOMY & FUNCTIONS

Know the tissue.
Understand the disease.

Two connective and contractile systems work together to produce controlled movement and maintain joint stability.

〰
CONTRACTILE TISSUE

Skeletal Muscle

Bundles of long, multinucleated muscle fibers arranged in fascicles. Actin–myosin interaction generates contraction and movement.

  • Produces movement and posture
  • Generates heat
  • Supports metabolic homeostasis
⌁
DENSE CONNECTIVE TISSUE

Ligaments

Collagen-rich bands connecting bone to bone. Their organized fibers resist excessive joint movement and guide stability.

  • Stabilize joints
  • Limit abnormal motion
  • Provide proprioceptive feedback
✦

Pathology connection: Injury may involve myofibers, fascia, tendon, enthesis (the tendon/ligament-to-bone attachment), synovial structures or the joint itself. Accurate sampling and anatomical localization are essential.

04 / THE PATHOLOGY LENS

Look closer.
Lesions tell the story.

Gross pathology and histopathology are central to this project. The two views complement each other: one reveals distribution and pattern, the other reveals cellular detail.

◉MACROSCOPIC EXAMINATION

Gross Lesions

Assess color, size, shape, consistency, distribution, exudate, hemorrhage, necrosis, mineralization and tissue architecture.

EXTERNAL / CUT SURFACE
Record the location, extent and character of every visible lesion.
⌕MICROSCOPIC EXAMINATION

Histopathological Lesions

Evaluate myofiber degeneration or necrosis, inflammatory-cell type, vascular changes, fibrosis, parasites and tissue repair.

H&E-STYLE MICROSCOPIC PATTERN
Describe the tissue, stain, magnification and diagnostic feature.

Academic accuracy note

Rheumatoid arthritis, systemic lupus erythematosus/Jaccoud arthropathy and inclusion body myositis are primarily established human disease entities. Veterinary relevance is species-dependent. Present comparative human pathology separately from documented animal diseases and cite species-specific evidence wherever available.
